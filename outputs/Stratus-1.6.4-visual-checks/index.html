Known limits

  • Screen-space water reflections cannot reconstruct hidden or off-screen geometry.
  • Only the nearest visible underwater exit is captured; disconnected water bodies along the ray are not accumulated.
  • Missing sky exits keep the existing clear-sky fallback.
  • Moisture uses material/face/sky heuristics, not persistent wetness or a geometric crevice simulation.
  • Inside cloud layers the integration range smoothly approaches max(512, 4*CLOUD_SPAN): 960 blocks at default span. More distant interior structure is approximated by the background; low sampling can still soften fine detail.
  • Visual coverage is finite and uses still-frame sequences. No continuous-motion recording, exhaustive mod/world coverage or verified foreground performance is claimed.

Rejected empty/misplaced and experimental frames are recorded in results.json and excluded from the passing final-source views. The blue concrete in the waterfall fixture is a deliberately coloured block.
