Inspected results

  • Top, opposite side and underside faces are distinct; warm ground shifts stone and wood gently without changing the indoor/night warmth probe.
  • Entities remain dark in an unlit room and take on lantern lighting when lit; ordinary white blocks/entities do not acquire an emissive halo.
  • Default bloom spreads visibly beyond lantern cores, with a broader softer radius than 0.50. Firefly bushes retain their correct texture using the existing compatibility resource pack.
  • Water is visibly denser and blue-green; clarity 4.00 is more transparent. Top, side and underwater/interface views remain usable; ripple normals bend the transmitted scene.
  • A lake seen through foreground clouds is obscured along with the surrounding terrain. The clouds-off comparison reveals the lake; this uses the lower cloud setting to fit the eight-chunk test view.
  • Snowball, egg, pearl, block-destruction fragments, smoke, rain and translucent slime render without the prior billboard-normal shading defect.
  • Lowland mist is height-dependent and spatially varied with a clear nearby view; its terrain relationship is an approximation based on height, visible depth and skylight.
  • The warm solar location is visible from ground level in morning/noon overcast views; sunset warms the sky. Clouds remain visible at night without daytime sun emission.
  • Main clouds show rounded density, shading, inside-layer extinction and parallax. Upper wisps appear from below, within and above; lower sampling retains both layers.
  • Held-item lighting, blindness, lava immersion, Nether and End rendering were inspected. The Nether frame was recaptured after loading cleared.
  • Half-resolution reconstruction can soften very thin boundaries. Water reflection/refraction and fog use the real-time approximations documented in README.txt.
  • A striped shallow pool was inspected with refraction on, moving and off; the transmitted stripe boundaries bend subtly with the animated normals, while the control is straight. No geometry waves are added.

Practical limits

  • Finite scenarios, not an exhaustive block/mod/world guarantee
  • Isolated Iris/Sodium/test-helper client; normal-profile mod stack not reproduced
  • Normal Minecraft client also running; shared GPU/CPU and thermal contention
  • Native UI inventory reported the Mac locked during measurement; active-play timing may differ
  • 20-second wall-interval samples, not isolated GPU timings; stutters affect lows
  • No guarantee of locked 60 fps or native Retina resolution performance
  • Mist follows height, 3D density and depth/skylight, not a simulated terrain humidity field
  • Approximate cloud scattering and prefiltered reflection sky; half-resolution thin-edge detail loss
  • Screen-space water cannot recover hidden geometry; approximate partial transparency and bounded waterfall thickness
  • Class-based estimated roughness and porosity, no measured PBR maps or cast-shadow maps
