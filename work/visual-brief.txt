## 2. Lighting and ambient occlusion

**Do not implement directional cast shadows for the reference appearance.** Trees, railings, terrain, and lanterns must not project shadows onto other surfaces. Disable shadow-map rendering across the supplied presets.

Use diffuse daytime illumination. Surfaces should remain readable beneath the cloud cover, including the sides of wooden posts and terrain blocks.

Retain gentle vanilla block-face shading so that top and side faces remain distinguishable. Use weak ambient occlusion only at nearby intersections and corners.

The preview’s AO is subdued:

- Railing joints have slight contact darkening.
- Terrain steps retain depth without dark outlines.
- Foliage does not become a mass of black creases.
- The wooden platform remains evenly readable.

Do not interpret “reduced AO” as a global exposure increase. Adjust contact shading independently.

Lantern interiors should be amber-orange with pale yellow centres and recognisable pixel detail. Any illumination on nearby blocks must be diffuse. Do not add reflected lantern images, strong orange pools, cast shadows, or large glow halos.

Do not make the lake perfectly mirror-flat or fill it with large waves and bright sparkling highlights.

Use greater transparency near shallow shores and reduced visibility through deeper water. The exact absorption depth is not measurable from the image; tune it against the reference.

## 8. Implementation guidance — suggestions, not observations

Inspect Willow Lite and the installed Iris environment before choosing the pipeline. Implement the visual targets above without assuming the reference used any particular technique.

Suitable approaches include:

- Diffuse skylight, vanilla face shading, and weak contact AO without a shadow pass.
- A small number of low-frequency procedural noise layers for clouds.
- Gentle domain warping and directional scaling to create irregular cloud bands.
- Distance fog combined with height-dependent density and broad spatial variation for valley mist.
- World-space water normals generated from a small number of wave or noise layers.
- Fresnel-weighted water reflections with depth-dependent absorption.
- Screen-space terrain reflections with a smooth sky/cloud fallback.
- Consistent linear-light calculations and a restrained final tone curve.

Prevent outdoor fog from leaking excessively into enclosed caves. Handle transparent materials and water depth correctly.

Do not add expensive effects that are not needed to match the image.
