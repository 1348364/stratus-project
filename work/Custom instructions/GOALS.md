# Shader priorities

These are standing preferences, not an instruction to overhaul the whole shader in every task.

- Believable water with clear reflections, refraction, depth, tint and turbidity appropriate to the environment.
- Consistent transitions between above-water and underwater views, including shorelines, waterfalls and transparent blocks.
- Natural lighting, atmosphere and clouds that fit Minecraft's visual style and the user's references.
- Practical performance on the user's hardware, measured under consistent conditions.
- Useful shader controls that preserve existing options and sensible defaults.
- Reliable, reproducible packaging and honest separation of technical checks from actual in-game verification.

Current release, settings and unresolved defects must be checked from the live project; this file does not certify a build.
